FOCUS

Focus, on your terms.

One local extension: declarative network filtering for ads and trackers, plus a deliberate blocker for short-form reels. No account, no cloud, no analytics.

Protection

Master switch for all network filtering and cosmetic hiding.

Betting sites

Blocks a curated list of sportsbooks, casinos and poker rooms as whole sites — address bar, bookmark, search result or link. The page is replaced by the browser's blocked-page notice. Filtering of everything else is unaffected.

Need one back? Add its hostname to Allowed sites below; the allowlist outranks the betting block.

Reels blocker

Hides reels entry points and replaces a reel feed with a pause screen. A five-minute pass is always available.

Reels blocking is off until you approve it. Ads and trackers are filtered either way.

Allowed sites

One host per line. Subdomains are included. Allowed sites are exempt from both network filtering and the reels blocker.
