Reels blocker needs your approval.

FOCUS can hide short-form entry points and replace a reel feed with a pause screen. It only does that after you turn it on here.

What it will do on these sites

    What it will not do

    • Read, capture or change the content of any page.
    • Touch your account, messages or cookies.
    • Send anything anywhere. No account, no server, no analytics.
    • Block sign-in, sign-up, password or account-recovery screens.

    Ad and tracker filtering is separate and already running. Reels blocking stays off, including after an update, until you approve it — and you can switch it off again in the popup or in Settings.

    Stored locally in this browser. Nothing here is sent to a server.
