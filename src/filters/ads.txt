! Curated ad-server, ad-exchange and ad-path rules compiled into the `ads`
! declarativeNetRequest ruleset at build time. Not EasyList: every line here was
! chosen deliberately and can be read in one sitting.
!
! Ground rules for this file:
!   - never block an account, login, recovery or payment endpoint;
!   - never block a general CDN (assets are needed for sites to render);
!   - prefer a whole ad domain over a path rule on a useful host;
!   - keep cosmetic selectors narrow and scoped to a domain.
