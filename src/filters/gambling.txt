! FOCUS betting-site rules
! Compiled into the separate `gambling` ruleset, which is the only ruleset
! allowed to block a top-level navigation. It is switched on and off with the
! "Betting sites" setting; ads and trackers are filtered independently.
!
! `$all` is used on purpose: it blocks the site as a document (typing the
! address, a bookmark, a search result, a link from anywhere) and also blocks
! its scripts, iframes and widgets when they are embedded in another page.
!
! The user allowlist still wins: pausing a domain writes an allow rule at
! priority 1000, above these blocks at 100. Add a site in Settings → Allowed
! sites if you need it back.
!
! Scope: licensed and unlicensed sportsbooks, casinos and poker rooms. Nothing
! here is a news, travel or hotel site. Domains are the consumer front doors;
! affiliate and comparison sites are deliberately not included.
