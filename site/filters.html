Every entry below ships in src/filters/ as plain text and is compiled at build time into declarative rules. The extension downloads nothing at runtime — if a domain is not on this page, this version does not block it.

Syntax is a conservative subset of uBlock Origin filters: ||domain^ blocks a domain and its subdomains, domain##selector hides matching elements on that site, and $all extends a rule to the document itself.

What is deliberately not here

  • No account, login, recovery or payment endpoint is ever blocked.
  • No general-purpose CDN — sites need those assets to render at all.
  • No regular expressions, scriptlets or procedural filters; the compiler rejects them.
  • No first-party path rule on a host people need, unless it is an ad path.
