Browser extension · v0.4.0

Quiet over the loud parts.

One Manifest V3 extension for Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers. Filter lists are compiled at build time into declarative rules; the browser enforces them, not a script watching your traffic.

What it blocks

Network

Ads and trackers

135 ad rules and 62 tracker rules covering the major exchanges, analytics SDKs and conversion pixels, plus generic ad-slot hiding in Complete mode.

Sponsored

Feed ads

47 site-specific selectors for sponsored posts and ad slots on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, X, Reddit, LinkedIn, Twitch, Spotify and Amazon.

Sites

Betting

63 sportsbooks, casinos and poker rooms blocked as whole sites. Its own switch, and your allowlist always outranks it.

See the full rule list — every domain and selector, generated from the files the build compiles.

Reels, with permission first

The reels blocker covers Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok’s For You feed and Snapchat Spotlight. Entry points disappear before the first paint; opening a reel route shows a pause screen with a five-minute pass.

It is the only feature that changes what a page shows, so it is opt-in. A permission screen lists the five sites on install, nothing is hidden until you approve it, and an update can never switch it back on behind your back. If a reel was opened from the feed, “Back to feed” walks the site’s own history — no reload, no signed-out shell.

Filtering modes

Basic
Ad rules only. No cosmetic hiding.
Optimal (default)
Ads, trackers, and per-site sponsored-slot hiding.
Complete
Also applies the generic ad-slot selectors on every site.
Betting sites
Separate switch, on by default, independent of the mode.

Permissions

storage
Your settings, allowlist and daily counter, in your browser profile.
declarativeNetRequest
Applies the packaged filter rules. No request bodies, headers or cookies are read.
tabs
Reads the active tab’s hostname for the popup’s site control, and opens the extension’s own permission page after install.
Content scripts
A stylesheet on any site for cosmetic hiding; the reels blocker only on the five platforms. No host_permissions are requested.

What it cannot do

  • Skip YouTube pre-roll or mid-roll ads. It hides in-feed and display slots; skipping in-player video needs techniques this build does not ship.
  • Block a site nobody added to the list. The lists are hand-curated and readable.
  • Stop you from switching it off. That is your call.
