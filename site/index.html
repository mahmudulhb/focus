How the app works

One promise, both products

  • No account. Nothing to sign in to, nothing to sync.
  • No analytics. No telemetry, no tracking SDK, no ads inside the product.
  • Nothing leaves the device. The extension has no server; the Android app declares no internet permission at all.
  • It asks first. Reels blocking is opt-in and the Android app explains each permission before you grant it.
  • It tells you what it cannot do. No fake ad-blocking percentage, no invented focus score.
